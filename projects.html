Research

LAAS-CNRS, Gepetto and MEMS teams, May – Jul 2026

Spiking neural network control of the Unitree H1-2

A simulator of a 3D cortical culture grown on microelectrode arrays, trained with biologically plausible learning rules as a closed-loop controller. 100% survival in simulation, then first steps on the physical robot driven directly by the network. First-author paper at IEEE-RAS Humanoids 2026.

LAAS-CNRS, Gepetto team, Jul – Aug 2026

Model-predictive control and imitation learning for the Unitree R1

A sampling-based cross-entropy MPC in MuJoCo MJX, eight quantitative gait metrics, and a DeepMimic-style PPO policy tracking commanded velocities up to 0.63 m/s.
