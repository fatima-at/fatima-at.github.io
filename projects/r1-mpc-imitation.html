Projects / MPC and imitation learning

Model-predictive control and imitation learning for the Unitree R1

A locomotion pipeline for the Unitree R1 humanoid in MuJoCo MJX: a sampling-based model-predictive controller to generate gaits, quantitative metrics to select them, and a reinforcement-learning policy that imitates the selected gait in real time.

Where
LAAS-CNRS, Gepetto team
When
Jul – Aug 2026
Role
Research intern
Tools
MuJoCo MJX, JAX, PPO

About the lab

LAAS-CNRS (Toulouse) is a laboratory of CNRS, France's national scientific research centre, and one of its largest in systems engineering, from robotics and control to micro- and nano-systems.

Its Gepetto team works on motion generation and control for legged and humanoid robots, and develops widely used open-source tools such as Pinocchio and Crocoddyl.

Method

  • Implemented a sampling-based cross-entropy method (CEM) model-predictive controller in MuJoCo MJX, with parallel rollouts on the GPU.
  • Derived the target gait period analytically from the compound-pendulum parameters of the robot's legs.
  • Defined eight quantitative gait metrics and used them to identify and eliminate a hopping gait.
  • Generated a reference dataset from the selected gait and trained a DeepMimic-style imitation policy with Proximal Policy Optimization.

Results

  • 0.63 m/smaximum commanded velocity tracked in real time
  • 544 stepsof walking (about 11 s) with the learned policy
  • 110 steps(2.2 s) for open-loop replay of the same reference
